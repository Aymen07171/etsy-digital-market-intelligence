### Core Pinterest Execution Pillars
1. **Bundle Engineering for Paid Ad Viability:** Never run cold Pinterest Paid Ads directly to `$4–$8` single downloads. Package 4–8 related files into an **All-in-One System / Mega Bundle (`$22–$37`)** so your allowable CPA (`$8.00–$13.20`) easily clears Pinterest auction CPCs (`$0.15–$0.35`).
2. **9:16 Video & Interactive Walkthrough Pins:** For Notion, GoodNotes, Bookkeeping Spreadsheets, and Procreate Brushes, use 12–15 second `9:16` screen-capture or iPad stylus demo videos showing the *before vs. after* workflow transformation.
3. **Etsy Share & Save Attribution:** Always use `shopname.etsy.com` or tracked Share & Save URLs as the destination link on every organic and paid Pin to reclaim `4%` of every order value from Etsy fees.
4. **Lead Magnet Bridge Page for Repeat LTV:** Capture email + Pinterest Pixel remarketing audiences using a free 1-page sample template (e.g., *Free ADHD Daily Brain Dump* or *Free LLC Write-Off Checklist*) that immediately redirects to a time-sensitive `30% OFF` Etsy bundle coupon.
